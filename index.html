3D Interactive Building Model

Turn a department program into a 3D stacking diagram. Move departments between floors, keep floor plates within capacity, and present the result to your client.

  1. 01

    Load a program

    Departments and areas from an Excel workbook or CSV, or the fictional demo campus.

  2. 02

    Stack it

    Drag departments between levels and add levels as the design grows. A floor that is over capacity turns red.

  3. 03

    Present it

    Switch between 3D Massing, Axon and Section Stack, and guide the room with the red laser pointer.
